Chicken Carbonara Noodles

A tasty dish full of savory, from the heart of Italy

Ingredients;

- 3 large eggs

- ¾ cup of grated parmesan cheese, plus more for serving

- ½ cup of heavy cream

- 1 tsp. of black pepper, plus more for taste

- 4 slices thick-cut diced bacon

- 1 lb. of skinless chicken breasts, sliced crosswise into ½-inch-thick strips

- 1 tsp. kosher salt, plus more for the pasta water

- 2 garlic cloves, minced

- 12 oz. fettuccine or linguine

- ½ cup frozen peas

- chopped fresh parsley for the garnish.


INSTRUCTIONS:

1. Whisk the eggs, parmesan, heavy cream, and pepper together in a bowl. Then set aside

2. Cook the bacon in a skillet at a medium heat. Flip to cook on both sides until crispy and fat rendered. (5-7 minutes.) Transfer to plate after cooked

3. Increase heat to medium-high to cook chicken in skillet. Season with salt and remaining ½ of pepper. Flip to cook on both sides until browned and cooked through. (6-8 minutes.) In last minute of cooking, stir in the garlic then transfer to plate. DO NOT WIPE OUT SKILLET!

4. Bring large pot of salted water to a boil. Add pasta noodles, and cook according to the package directions. Add peas in last minute. Reserve 1 cup of cooking water, then drain the rest

5. Immediately add the hot pasta and peas to the skillet off the heat. Pour in the carbonara sauce and quickly toss with tongs, adding 2-3 tablespoons of the reserved cooking water, until the sauce is creamy. If the sauce is too thick, add more of the reserved cooking water. If it’s too thin, cook over low heat until thickened

6. Stir in the bacon pieces and sliced chicken. Garnish with parsley, pepper and parmesan

Blow to cool, then bite
